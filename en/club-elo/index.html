Women's club Elo ratings

Elo ratings for 312 women's clubs, computed in date order from 1,864 results in 22 leagues and three UEFA competitions. Bayern München lead on 1929, ahead of FC Barcelona (1908) and Manchester City (1907). By median club rating the strongest league is the NWSL, followed by the Women's Super League and the Frauen-Bundesliga. Ratings use results only; the gap between leagues is adjusted only through European cup matches. Leagues with no European cup matches (NWSL, Women's Super League 2, Liga MX Femenil (Apertura), Northern Super League, WE League, A-League Women, USL Super League and WK League) are placed by their starting rating from the Opta rank alone.

How to read it. The rating gap between two clubs gives the stronger side's expected share of points on a neutral ground: 100 points → 64%, 200 → 76%, 400 → 91% (a win counts 1, a draw half). The absolute number means nothing on its own; only differences matter. The scale is not the same as men's Elo lists or Opta Power Rankings.

Method

  1. Starting point. Every club in a league starts on the same rating: the league ranked r in Opta's women's league power ranking gets 1700 − 30·(r − 1) (1st NWSL 1700, 20th WK League 1130). Leagues outside Opta's top 20, and European cup teams whose league is not in this data, start on 1050. The 30-point step and 1050 were chosen by pre-match prediction accuracy in European cup matches, the only information linking leagues (a 10-point step predicted clearly worse).
  2. Expected result. E = 1 / (1 + 10^(−(R_home + H − R_away) / 400)); a win counts 1, a draw 0.5, a loss 0.
  3. Update. Δ = K · G · (S − E). K is 60 in a club's first 8 matches (so ratings move away from the starting value quickly), then 30; a match uses the average of the two clubs' K. G is the goal-difference multiplier (eloratings.net rule): margin 0-1 → 1, 2 → 1.5, 3 or more → (11 + margin) / 8.
  4. Home advantage. H = 25 points, measured from the home side's share of points in all league matches (53.3%). In cup matches H goes to the club whose known ground hosts the match; otherwise to the home side in two-legged ties and the Champions League league phase. Neutral-venue qualifying mini-tournament games (39) get no H.
  5. Cross-league calibration only through European cups. When clubs from different leagues meet, 50% of Δ goes to the base rating of the club's league, i.e. to every club in it, and the rest to the club itself. League matches are zero-sum and do not move a league's average. Leagues without European cup matches (NWSL, Women's Super League 2, Liga MX Femenil (Apertura), Northern Super League, WE League, A-League Women, USL Super League and WK League) are placed relative to each other and to Europe by their starting rating alone. Women's football has few cross-league matches; this calibration is the weakest link.
  6. Excluded. Forfeits: 3 matches marked as awarded or no-show by the TFF, and 8 3-0 / 0-3 results without a SoccerDonna match report (possibly awarded). The other 112 results without a report are counted; with no date, they are ordered by their matchweek's date (the Last match column uses dated matches only).
  7. Ranking. Clubs with at least 5 matches are ranked; others are marked provisional. The 30-day column is the change since 30 days before the latest dated match (12 November 2026).
  8. Accuracy. Each result was predicted with the ratings as they stood before the match: Brier score 0.169 (0.151 in European cup matches), against 0.205 using home advantage alone. Lower is better.
